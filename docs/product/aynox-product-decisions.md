# Aynox Product Decision Register

**Status:** Living register. Confirmed entries define product direction; Open entries require evidence or founder approval.  
**Purpose:** Keep the startup roadmap, MVP PRD, and milestone product/technical designs consistent. This register does not claim any feature is implemented.  
**Authority:** The MVP PRD specifies confirmed product requirements; milestone PRDs/designs specify proposed expansions; the roadmap specifies sequence; this register records the decision state.

| ID | Status | Decision | Chosen direction | Reasoning | Development implications | Remaining validation |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| D-01 | Confirmed | Product purpose | AI-powered investment research and portfolio intelligence, launching from Qatar | This is the agreed product direction | Build research and tracking workflows, not an account or trading system | None for product direction |
| D-02 | Confirmed | Target audiences | Beginner investors; existing retail investors; university students and young professionals | Confirmed audiences need approachability plus analytical depth | Use clear explanations and progressive analytical detail | Prioritize a first pilot use case |
| D-03 | Confirmed | Market scope | Global stocks introduced progressively as coverage, rights, availability, cost, and quality are verified | Avoid implying all global listings are supported | Maintain explicit supported-security catalogue and coverage states; keep the approved six-market proof of concept deferred until the appropriate development stage | Exact initial exchanges/securities and timing of the deferred proof of concept |
| D-04 | Confirmed | Market-data presentation | Show exchange, currency, market status, provider/source, timestamp, and freshness for supported securities; daily portfolio intervals follow each security’s exchange-specific trading calendar | Context is needed to interpret global market data and align daily prices | Preserve exchange calendar and market/session metadata throughout data and UI | Numeric freshness/update targets and exact cross-exchange session-date mapping are open |
| D-05 | Open | Market-data provider and rights | Start with synthetic/mock market data behind a provider-neutral adapter; Twelve Data and Finnhub are candidates for later adapters; final production provider remains unselected | Provider terms, coverage, latency, cost, and data quality determine feasible scope | Keep provider integration replaceable; never display restricted development API data to production customers without appropriate commercial rights | Select production provider and verify coverage, entitlements, commercial terms, cost, and quality; confirm applicable development-data restrictions |
| D-06 | Confirmed | Portfolio meaning | Manually selected stocks for monitoring and analysis; not owned holdings | No holdings or brokerage data is collected | Persist symbols only; avoid ownership semantics | None for core definition |
| D-07 | Confirmed | Portfolio index math | Equal-weighted daily rebalanced synthetic index; each interval return is the mean eligible constituent daily price return; compound from index value 100 | This is the confirmed calculation basis | r(i,t)=P(i,t)/P(i,t-1)-1; r(p,t)=mean over eligible i of r(i,t); I(t)=I(t-1)*(1+r(p,t)); missing valid prices are excluded and eligible stocks equally reweighted with coverage disclosure, never zero-imputed | Verify implementation with deterministic cases and exchange-calendar fixtures |
| D-08 | Confirmed | Return basis | Use verified provider-adjusted prices that account for stock splits; exclude cash dividends from the initial price-return index | Preserve the approved price-return methodology | Label index price-return-only; it is never user P&L or actual investment return; verify adjusted-close semantics and do not assume dividends are excluded | Verify selected provider field and evidence for splits, dividend exclusion, symbol changes, and other relevant corporate actions |
| D-09 | Confirmed | Tracked-list membership history | Record add/remove effective timestamps; new stocks enter from the next eligible calculation interval; removed stocks stop contributing from their effective removal interval; preserve historical membership and never recalculate past composition from the current list | Prevents selection changes from rewriting prior index performance | Persist effective membership intervals and calculate each historical interval using its then-current eligible constituents | Define implementation details for timestamp timezone/boundaries and mapping across exchange-specific sessions; do not change the confirmed historical-preservation rule |
| D-10 | Confirmed | Stock forecasting | Forecasts require out-of-sample evaluation; show horizons, uncertainty, limits, and data timestamps | Avoid unsupported accuracy or performance claims | Chronological holdout/rolling evaluation and baseline; gate user-facing performance claims | Model, horizons, protocol, metric thresholds, release gate remain open |
| D-11 | Confirmed | Initial news-source direction | Improve Yahoo Finance company-news relevance filtering | Yahoo Finance news is the intended initial source | Implement company matching, relevance filtering, deduplication, source attribution | Verify access, rights, availability, storage/summary permissions |
| D-12 | Confirmed | News intelligence | Relevant company coverage, article-level sentiment, company-level aggregates, structured source-attributed reports | A raw feed alone is insufficient | Preserve canonical links, article/company mapping, sentiment and report version | Evaluation criteria, method, freshness and relevance thresholds open |
| D-13 | Confirmed | Sentiment meaning | Descriptive analysis; does not prove future stock movement | Avoid implying causal or predictive performance | Label method/coverage and prevent predictive sentiment claims | Validate language and model behavior |
| D-14 | Confirmed | ESG standard | Transparent source, methodology, scores and comparison context; unverified scores are not objective facts | ESG sources/scales may differ or be incomplete | Store provider/date/scale/method; suppress unsupported comparison | Provider, rights, peer groups, updates, missing-data and derived-score rules open |
| D-15 | Confirmed | Community placement | Community posts and interaction are in the vision, sequenced in Phase 4 after the Phase 3 controlled MVP pilot | Pilot should evaluate core research and portfolio intelligence before extensions are prioritized | Keep outside Phase 1–2 and Phase 3 pilot scope | Exact interaction, access, moderation and service requirements open |
| D-16 | Confirmed | Monitoring | Dashboard-based monitoring prioritized initially; advanced alerts are later | Keeps first experience focused and achievable | Display latest data and freshness; alerts not Phase 1–2 blockers | Dashboard refresh behavior and any later alert case open |
| D-17 | Confirmed | Development sequence | Phase 0 evidence, rights, and feasibility; Phase 1 core investment research; Phase 2 portfolio intelligence; Phase 3 controlled MVP pilot; Phase 4 community and extensions | Pilot must evaluate the core experience before extensions are prioritized | Align roadmap, PRD, and release gates to phases | Approve any deviation and pilot release cut |
| D-18 | Confirmed | Product exclusions | No brokerage connection, actual holding sync, or actual investment accounting; no trading/accounting requirements | Aynox is a research platform, not an investment account | No quantities, cost basis, holdings, or actual P&L in current scope | Any future change requires a new founder decision |
| D-19 | Open | Initial pilot user/job | Confirmed audiences remain in scope; one initial pilot task is not selected | Product research should focus the first evaluation without narrowing the audience definition | Prioritize interview/research workflow and onboarding | Select the first workflow and audience priority from evidence |
| D-20 | Open | Quote/news freshness | No numeric thresholds approved | Source terms and market sessions affect viable latency | Show observed timestamps/stale states; avoid unsupported “real-time” claims | Set quote/news latency, refresh cadence, and market-session rules |
| D-21 | Open | Forecast release gate | Out-of-sample evidence is required; no numeric pass/fail threshold approved | Forecast quality depends on asset, horizon, market and baseline | Keep forecasts gated until criteria are approved and met | Approve target, horizons, baselines, metrics, window, calibration and threshold |
| D-22 | Open | News relevance/sentiment evaluation | No evaluation set, method, or quality threshold approved | Quality claims require labeled evidence | Evaluate matching, dedup, sentiment, and report grounding before claims | Approve sample, language scope, labels, metrics, criteria, rights and freshness |
| D-23 | Open | ESG data/comparison | No provider, peer group, or derived-score method approved | Coverage and scale compatibility affect meaningful comparisons | Distinguish provider values from derived insight; suppress unsupported ranks | Select provider, rights, scale, peers, update schedule, aggregation |
| D-24 | Open | Community operations | Phase 4 placement is confirmed; detailed interaction/moderation scope is not | Public interaction creates safety and support obligations | Do not make it a Phase 1–3 dependency; staff before public release | Approve visibility, posts/comments/reactions, reporting, moderator, escalation and response expectations |
| D-25 | Open | Identity and privacy operations | **Approved product scope:** the controlled MVP pilot includes real authenticated accounts and private, server-persisted watchlists; each operation must be authorized against the user’s verified identity. The synthetic browser-local demo remains a separate experience, and importing its tracking data must be explicit and user-approved. The watchlist is for research, not owned holdings. | The controlled pilot requires account-backed private tracking, while provider, identity lifecycle, privacy, and operations choices remain unresolved | Implement trusted identity resolution and server-side owner-scoped reads/writes; preserve separate demo state; validate any approved import as untrusted input | Select authentication provider/approach, registration policy, verification/MFA, session/recovery policy, database host and migration ownership, import details, export/deletion/retention, analytics/notices, data region, and operational owners |
| D-26 | Open | Platform/accessibility matrix | Responsive web is the working assumption; exact support matrix is pending | Defines UX and QA coverage | Include keyboard and responsive requirements from Phase 1 | Approve browser/device matrix, minimum viewport, accessibility review target |
| D-27 | Open | Pilot measures | Product measures are proposed, not approved | Go/no-go requires defined cohorts and windows | Instrument privacy-conscious events after metric approval | Approve definitions, cohorts, thresholds, duration, owner |
| D-28 | Open | Operational targets/ownership | Proposed latency/availability targets and incident ownership are unapproved | Targets must reflect pilot traffic and provider dependencies | Monitor freshness, outages, support and incidents | Approve SLO measurement, service owner, support channel and escalation |

| D-29 | Confirmed | Portfolio index currency methodology | Compute each constituent’s daily price return in its local trading currency, equally weight those percentage returns, and exclude FX movements; disclose that the index is not a common-currency or actual globally invested portfolio return | This is the approved initial methodology | Preserve each security’s local currency; do not convert returns or imply actual investment performance | A future common-currency index is outside the initial methodology and may be reconsidered only if reliable FX data and an approved method become available |
| D-30 | Confirmed | Historical price and corporate-action semantics | Use only a verified provider-adjusted historical price field that accounts for stock splits, excludes cash dividends for the initial index, and handles symbol changes and other relevant corporate actions consistently; never assume adjusted close excludes dividends | Verified series semantics are required for meaningful price returns | Document the selected provider field contract and preserve corporate-action lineage | After provider selection, verify field-specific split/dividend behavior, symbol-change continuity, other action coverage, and representative evidence before production use |
| D-31 | Open | AI provider, model, and evidence-processing terms | No provider or model is selected. OpenAI is recommended as the first structured-output benchmark adapter; the exact model remains subject to evaluation. | Schema-first generation and TypeScript integration suit the proposed validation pipeline, but model quality, cost, privacy, and vendor terms must be assessed with Aynox evidence fixtures | Keep a replaceable server-side AI adapter; transmit only evidence explicitly permitted for model processing; do not treat schema compliance as factuality | Approve benchmark provider/model, evaluation protocol, cost ceiling, data retention/region, source-data AI rights, and customer disclosure |
| D-32 | Open | Fundamentals data provider and coverage | No production fundamentals provider is selected. Finnhub is recommended as the first feasibility candidate when the approved development stage arrives; Twelve Data remains a comparator. | Finnhub publicly lists global standardized annual/quarterly statements, 30+ years, as-reported financials, and enterprise redistribution terms; exact rights, coverage, and costs remain unverified for Aynox | Build a provider-neutral fundamentals adapter; begin with synthetic/mock fixtures; respect the deferred proof-of-concept stage and do not display restricted development data to production customers | Verify representative supported securities, periods, currencies, metric definitions, restatement/update behavior, display/derivative/AI-processing/storage rights, and written pricing/entitlements; coordinate with D-05 |
| D-33 | Open | Research report generation, access, refresh, and retention | Recommended initial behavior is user-triggered report generation/refresh with permitted caching and immutable versions; user sharing is not approved | Limits unnecessary model calls and preserves a trace of the evidence/model used for each report | Store report/evidence versions only for contractually permitted periods; enforce user authorization and generation limits | Approve generation trigger, freshness/refresh behavior, who may access reports, version history, sharing, retention/deletion, and cost/rate limits |
| D-34 | Open | Financial period normalization and initial metric definitions | Proposed v1 catalogue covers revenue growth, margins, operating cash flow, free cash flow, current ratio, debt-to-equity, and P/E; distinguish discrete quarter, YTD, annual, TTM, and instant observations; derive quarters from YTD only under verified compatibility rules | Fiscal calendars, provider fields, accounting conventions, and valuation alignment vary and can make apparently simple comparisons misleading | Preserve source period basis, fiscal calendar, exact dates, units/currencies and lineage; calculate deterministically with versioned formulas; suppress incompatible or undefined values | Approve field mappings, period conversion/TTM rules, accounting scope, capex/debt/net-income definitions, fiscal comparability, denominator/materiality rules, valuation alignment, and display rounding |

## Decision updates

For each Open item, record an owner, approver, evidence, decision date, and revisit trigger when it is resolved. Change status to Confirmed only after founder approval. Update this register and the related roadmap/PRD sections together.
